Privacy Policy

Dragonrush Apps · Last updated 7 October 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use your email address only to check whether you have been given access to an app.

How it's stored

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, which stores your account details securely. We don't sell, share or use your information for advertising.

What we don't do

  • We don't access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • We don't post anything on your behalf.

Removing your data

To have your account removed, contact the app owner and it will be deleted. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
